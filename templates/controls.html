⚔️ HOW TO PLAY ⚔️

🎮 BASIC CONTROLS

⚡ LIGHT ATTACK
Fast, accurate, low damage. Hard to punish.
💥 HEAVY ATTACK
Slow, high damage. Risky if misses.
✨ SPECIAL ATTACK
Powerful move. Costs 25% super meter.
💫 SUPER MOVE
Ultimate attack. Costs 100% super meter.
🛡️ BLOCK
Reduces incoming damage by 75%. Builds meter.
💨 DODGE
70% chance to avoid all damage. Costs 20% meter.
💚 HEAL
Restores health. 3‑turn cooldown.
🏃 FORWARD / BACK
Change range (Close / Medium / Far). Costs 5% meter.

⚔️ COMBAT MECHANICS

🎯 Accuracy & Miss Chance: Light attacks are very accurate; heavy and super moves can miss and leave you open to punishment.
✨ Critical Hits: Random chance to deal 50% bonus damage. Increased by combos.
🔥 Combos: Landing consecutive attacks builds a combo counter. Each hit adds +5% damage, and long combos trigger finisher bonuses.
🌀 Status Effects: Stun, Burn, Poison, Empower, Weaken, Armor, and more can turn the tide.
⚡ Super Meter: Builds when you deal or take damage. Spend it on specials, supers, dodges, or breaking stun.
📍 Positioning: Close range favors melee; far range favors ranged attacks. Use step forward/back to control space.
🩸 Rage Mode: Below 30% HP, you deal +10% damage and gain meter faster.
💪 Character Passives: Monkey King's next attack is guaranteed after a successful dodge. Percy gains armor when healing.

🏆 WIN CONDITIONS

• Reduce the enemy’s HP to 0 to win the round.

• Best of 3 rounds wins the match.

• Maintain win streaks for bonus damage!

• Choose between Monkey King (agile) or Percy Jackson (tanky).

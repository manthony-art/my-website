⚔️ ABOUT TEXT FIGHTER ⚔️

🎮 GAME OVERVIEW

Text Fighter is a text-based fighting game brought to life in 3D using Three.js. Choose between the agile Monkey King or the tanky Percy Jackson and battle through intense rounds of strategic combat.

✨ FEATURES

  • ✓ Deep combat system with status effects, combos, and positioning
  • ✓ Super meter system for special moves and supers
  • ✓ Unique character abilities and passives
  • ✓ AI with three difficulty levels (Easy/Normal/Hard)
  • ✓ Win streak system that rewards consistent victories
  • ✓ Rage mode for comeback potential
  • ✓ Combo finishers that reward long chains
  • ✓ Position‑based strategy (Close/Medium/Far)
  • ✓ Fully 3D arena with cel‑shaded visuals

🐒 MONKEY KING

Agile trickster who excels at evasion and mix‑ups. After a successful dodge, his next attack is guaranteed to hit. Perfect for players who like to outmanoeuvre opponents.

🌊 PERCY JACKSON

Tanky water controller who can heal and build water empowerment stacks. When Percy heals, he gains temporary armour, making him harder to take down. Ideal for players who prefer a defensive, sustaining playstyle.

🏆 TIPS & STRATEGY

  • 💡 Build super meter early to unleash powerful specials.
  • 💡 Watch your positioning – some moves only work at certain ranges.
  • 💡 Use combos to increase damage output and trigger finishers.
  • 💡 Block and dodge to avoid big damage and build meter.
  • 💡 Rage mode activates below 30% HP – use this for comebacks!
  • 💡 Maintain win streaks for bonus damage.
